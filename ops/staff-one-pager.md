# For the team at [Business name]: what the AI receptionist does

Print this or forward it to everyone who answers the phone.

**Nothing changes for calls you answer.** Your phone rings first, exactly as before. The receptionist only picks up when nobody answers within about [15] seconds, and outside opening hours.

**What callers hear when it answers**

"Thanks for calling [Business name]. This is [Kylie], an AI assistant, and this call may be recorded. How can I help you today?"

It then does one of four things:

1. Books a visit on the shared "Service visits" calendar, one-hour slots inside opening hours. It confirms the address and the problem and tells the caller someone will confirm before the visit.
2. Answers the usual questions (hours, services, price ranges, service area) from the sheet we agreed. It does not invent prices or promise arrival times.
3. Transfers to [hand-over number] when someone asks for a person, is upset, or has something it can't handle. **If your phone rings showing the receptionist's number, that is a transferred customer. Please pick up.**
4. Takes a message after hours and says someone will call back [first thing in the morning].

**What you receive**

Within a minute of every call, [owner email] [and owner mobile] get a short summary: who called, what they needed, what was booked or promised. Every call is also logged in the "Receptionist CRM" sheet, and the recording and transcript are available if a caller disputes something.

**What to do with a booking it made**

Treat it like any other appointment. If the time doesn't work, move it on the calendar and call the customer; the receptionist does not call customers.

**If something sounds wrong**

If a caller tells you the receptionist said something odd, note the time and tell [your name] at [your phone or email]. It gets fixed the same day, and the fix applies to every future call.

**Emergencies**

If a caller describes gas, smoke, sparks or flooding near electrics, the receptionist tells them to leave the area and call 911 first, then offers to transfer.
