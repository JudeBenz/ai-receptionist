How it fits

Your people answer first. It catches what they drop.

1 · Call rings

Your desk or your cell rings as normal.

2 · 15 seconds

Nobody picks up, or it’s after hours. The AI answers and says it’s an AI.

3 · It handles it

Books the job, answers the usual questions, takes the details.

4 · Or hands over

Anything it can’t answer goes to you or to voicemail. Never a dead end.

Nobody loses a job. If a human can answer, they should. This runs the gap: on a job, at lunch, after hours and on weekends.

The 15-second delay, the hand-over number and the hours are set per business.

Both creators stress the same two things. AI Master: answering only after the front desk misses the call for 15 seconds is a much easier sell, you are not replacing anyone. And always set the fallback to voicemail or a human, because a dead-end call is what makes an owner rip the system out two weeks in.
