The leak

Most calls to a small business go nowhere.

38%

of calls were answered by a person. The other six in ten rang out.

3 in 4

callers never try again after one unanswered call.

6 in 10

dial a competitor instead. Every one had a wallet out.

Source: 2024 study of 85 small businesses, as cited in the source videos. Verify the study before presenting.

Don’t tell the owner this is their number. Ask instead: “Do you actually miss many calls? About how many a day? What’s an average job worth? How many of those would have booked if someone answered?” Let them say the number. The study figures are there to show the pattern is normal, not to compute their loss for them.
