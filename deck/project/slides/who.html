Who it is for

Businesses where one missed call costs more than a month of service.

Three tests

A phone call is worth real money.

The caller is urgent. They won’t leave a voicemail and wait.

One lost customer costs more than the monthly fee.

HVAC

The air went out. Someone is coming today, or they call the next company.

Plumbing

Water in the basement. Nobody waits until tomorrow for a call back.

Law firms

A new client calls once. The first firm that answers usually gets the case.

Med spas, dental and auto detailing pass the same tests and appear in the source videos.

Whitney’s three tests, in her words. Her own clients: an HVAC owner at $997 a month and a law firm at $497 a month, found by asking her contacts “who do you know who knows a lawyer?” rather than cold outreach. If the owner in the room says it’s not their busy season, ask which other trades they’d pass you to.
