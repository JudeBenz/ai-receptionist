Trust and compliance

Built to protect your name, not just answer the phone.

It says it is an AI

In the greeting and whenever a caller asks. Required by bot-disclosure laws such as California’s.

Recording notice on every call

About a dozen states require all-party consent, so every call opens with it.

Inbound only

It answers people who call you. It never dials out or cold-calls, which keeps you clear of telemarketing rules.

Your data stays yours

Calls, bookings and records land in your calendar and CRM. Text follow-ups start only after carrier registration (10DLC).

It never guesses a price or a policy. Anything outside its script becomes a call-back or a transfer to you.

US rules as summarised in the source videos. Confirm the states you operate in with a lawyer before promising anything.

AI Master’s three operator rules: disclose AI when asked, open with the recording line, register 10DLC before any SMS (campaign review takes 10 to 15 days, so start it early). Inbound is far lower risk than outbound AI dialing under the TCPA. The “never guesses” line comes from his prompt rule: offer a call-back instead of inventing an answer, because a hallucinated price costs the client real money.
