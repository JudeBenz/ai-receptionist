1 · Find the leak (8 minutes. Ask, then stop talking. Write their exact words and numbers.)

  1. When the phone rings and you're under a sink, what happens to that call?
  2. Who answers today, and during which hours? (you, office, family, answering service)
  3. About how many calls come in on a normal day?
  4. How many of those go to voicemail or ring out? And nights and weekends?
  5. What is an average job worth? And an emergency call-out?
  6. Of the ones you miss, how many would have booked if someone had picked up?
  7. What have you tried already, and what did you not like about it?
  8. Which calls have to reach you right away, whatever the hour?
  9. How do you schedule jobs now? (paper, Google Calendar, Housecall Pro, Jobber, ServiceTitan)
  10. What do you need to know from a caller before you send someone?
  11. If we tried this for 30 days, what would make you say it was worth it?
  12. Besides you, who has a say in this?

Never do their math for them. Let them say what the missed calls cost.

2 · Demo (10 minutes)

  1. Say: "It answers as a made-up heating company. Picture your own name in the greeting."
  2. Have them call (978) 347-8475 from their own cell, on speaker, as a customer. Tell them to try to confuse it.
  3. Let them book a visit, then ask "are you a robot?" and ask for a person.
  4. Show three things on your screen: the visit on the calendar, the new row in the call log, the summary email.
  5. Ask: "What would your callers ask that it didn't handle?" Write it down. That is their FAQ.

3 · Offer and close (5 minutes)

  • $425 one time to build and test it on their existing number. Nothing goes live until they have called it and approved it.
  • Then one bill at month end: $297 including 900 minutes, plus 20¢ a minute beyond. Card on file. Month to month.
  • Live in about a week: one 30-minute kickoff call, then they test it before callers hear it.
  • It never claims to be a person and always says the call may be recorded. The greeting wording is theirs to approve. Inbound only.
  • Close: "Shall we do the kickoff Thursday or Friday?" Then fill page 2 together, now.
Your own numbers, for your eyes only. Each minute the receptionist talks costs you about 10 cents. A missed call runs about 3 minutes, so every call a day it answers costs you about $9 a month. If it's not a yes today

Leave the one-pager. Ask them to call the demo line again tonight, after hours. Agree the day you will follow up: ______________. Ask who else they know who misses calls.
