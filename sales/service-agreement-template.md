## 1. What the provider does

The provider sets up and runs an AI telephone receptionist ("the Service") on the client's phone number [number]. The Service answers calls that the client's staff do not answer within [15] seconds, and all calls outside the hours in Schedule A. It can take caller details, answer questions from the information the client supplies, book, move and cancel appointments on the client's calendar, transfer calls to [number], and send the client a summary of each call by [email / text].

## 2. What the client does

- Supplies and keeps current the business information in Schedule A (hours, services, prices, service area, policies, questions callers ask).
- Provides access to the calendar and contact list the Service writes to.
- Tells callers, where the law requires it, that calls may be recorded; the Service states this at the start of every call.
- Reviews the call summaries and tells the provider about any wrong answer within [2] business days.

## 3. Pilot

The first [30] days are a pilot. If by day [30] the Service has not booked at least one appointment for the client, the client may end this agreement with no fee owed. Otherwise the subscription in section 4 begins on day [31].

## 4. Fees

- Plan: [Growth] at $[497] per month, billed monthly in advance by [card / bank transfer], first payment due [day 31].
- The plan includes [unlimited inbound minutes under fair use / up to N minutes], one phone line, and changes to Schedule A within [2] business days of request.
- Set-up is included. Additional lines or locations are $[__] per month each.
- Prices can change with [30] days' written notice.

## 5. Term and cancellation

Month to month after the pilot. Either party may cancel with [14] days' written notice. On cancellation the provider removes the Service from the client's number within [2] business days and deletes call recordings and transcripts within [30] days unless the client asks for a copy first.

## 6. What the Service is and is not

- The Service is software. It discloses that it is an AI when asked. It may mishear, misunderstand, or fail to answer, and calls may be dropped.
- The Service does not give professional, legal, medical or emergency advice. It tells callers describing an emergency to call 911.
- The provider does not guarantee any number of bookings, any revenue, or that every call will be answered.
- The client remains responsible for the accuracy of Schedule A and for honouring appointments the Service books from it.

## 7. Data and recordings

Call audio, transcripts and summaries are stored with the provider's platform vendors ([Vapi, n8n, Google]) under their terms. The provider uses them only to run and improve the Service for the client, and does not sell or share them. The client owns its customer data and may request an export at any time.

## 8. Liability

To the extent the law allows, the provider's total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees the client paid in the [3] months before the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, including lost business from a missed or mishandled call.

## 9. General

This agreement is governed by the laws of [State]. It is the whole agreement between the parties and can only be changed in writing. Notices go to the email addresses above.
