# Northwind Heating and Air: business information

This file is what the receptionist is allowed to quote. If a fact is not in here, the receptionist does not know it.

## Hours and contact

- Open Monday to Friday 7:00 am to 6:00 pm, Saturday 8:00 am to 12:00 noon, closed Sunday.
- Emergency service outside those hours is available for existing customers at the emergency rate below.
- Office address: [street address], Haverhill, MA.
- Website: [website].

## Service area

The Merrimack Valley and North Shore within about 30 miles of Haverhill, including Lawrence, Andover, Methuen, Newburyport, Lowell and Salem. For addresses outside that area the receptionist takes a message and the office calls back.

## Services

- Air-conditioning repair and replacement.
- Furnace and boiler repair and replacement.
- Heat pumps and ductless mini-splits.
- Seasonal tune-ups and maintenance plans.
- Thermostat installation, including smart thermostats.
- Indoor air quality: filters, humidifiers, air purifiers.
- Duct cleaning is not offered. The receptionist takes a message and the office refers a partner.

## Pricing (ranges only, the technician confirms on site)

- Diagnostic visit: $89, waived if the repair is done the same day.
- Seasonal tune-up: $129 for one system, $199 for heating and cooling together.
- Emergency after-hours visit: $189 diagnostic.
- Common repairs: most fall between $150 and $600 for parts and labour.
- New air conditioner installed: typically $4,500 to $9,000 depending on size and efficiency.
- New furnace installed: typically $3,500 to $7,500.
- Maintenance plan: $19 a month, includes two tune-ups a year and 15 percent off repairs.

## Scheduling

- Service visits are booked in one-hour arrival windows.
- The technician calls or texts about 30 minutes before arriving.
- Someone over 18 must be home for the visit.
- Cancellations and changes are free with at least 4 hours' notice.

## Payment

- Cash, check, all major cards.
- Financing available on new systems through [finance partner]; the office explains the terms.

## Warranty

- Repairs carry a one-year parts and labour warranty.
- New systems carry the manufacturer's warranty plus two years of labour from Northwind.

## Brands

Carrier, Lennox, Trane, Goodman, Rheem and most other major brands are serviced. New installs are usually Carrier or Lennox.

## Things the receptionist should not promise

- An exact arrival time (only the one-hour window).
- An exact repair price before a technician has looked.
- Same-day service. It can book the earliest open slot and note that the caller wants the first cancellation.
