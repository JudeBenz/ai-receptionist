Privacy

Last updated November 12, 2026. Plain language on purpose.

Who we are

AfterRing sets up and runs AI phone receptionists for small businesses in Massachusetts. Questions about anything on this page: afterringsservices@gmail.com.

The demo request form

The form asks for your name, your business, a phone number, your type of business and a good time to call. We use those details for one thing: to call you about a demo. They arrive in our email inbox. We do not sell them, share them for marketing, or add you to a mailing list.

The demo phone line

Calls to (978) 347-8475 are answered by an AI assistant playing the receptionist of a made-up heating company. It says it is an AI and that the call may be recorded before anything else.

  • The call is recorded and transcribed, and we keep your phone number, the recording, the transcript and a short summary.
  • If you give a name or an address, or book a test visit, that is saved in our demo calendar and demo customer list. Use made-up details if you prefer; it works the same.
  • We use demo calls to show the service to business owners and to improve it. We never call or text the number back to sell you something.

Who handles the data for us

Vapi runs the phone line and the voice assistant, along with the speech and language services it relies on. n8n runs our automations. Google provides our email, calendar and spreadsheets. GitHub hosts this website and, like any web host, sees your IP address when you load a page. The fonts on this site load from Google Fonts.

What this site does not do

No advertising, no analytics, no tracking pixels, and no cookies set by us.

Callers to our clients' businesses

When a business uses AfterRing, its receptionist tells every caller that it is an AI assistant and that the call may be recorded. That call data belongs to the business you called and is used to serve you: to book the visit, pass on the message and send the business its summary. To ask about a call you made to one of our clients, contact that business, or write to us and we will pass it on.

Seeing or deleting your details

Email afterringsservices@gmail.com from any address, tell us the phone number you called from or the name you gave, and we will send you what we hold or delete it.
