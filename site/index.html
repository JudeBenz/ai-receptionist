How it works

Your people answer first. It catches what they drop.

STEP 1

The call rings as normal

Your desk or cell rings the way it does today. Nothing changes for calls you answer.

STEP 2

Fifteen seconds, or after hours

If nobody picks up, the receptionist answers in your business's name and says the call may be recorded.

STEP 3

It handles the call

Takes the details, answers the usual questions, checks your real calendar and books the visit.

STEP 4

Or hands it to you

Anything it can't answer goes to you or to voicemail. Never a dead end.

After every call

You see exactly what happened, without stopping work.

A summary by text or email

Who called, what they needed, what was agreed. Two or three sentences, within a minute of hanging up.

The full transcript and recording

Every word, with the caller's name, number and address, kept in your account.

Booked on your calendar

It checks real availability, books the slot, and can move or cancel it when the customer calls back.

Logged with your customers

New callers get a record. Returning callers are recognised by their number and greeted by name.

Questions owners ask

Straight answers.

Will my customers know it's an AI?

It never pretends to be a person. Anyone who asks is told plainly that it's an AI, and callers who want a person are transferred to you or get a call back. By default it also says so in its greeting; the wording of your greeting is yours to choose. Every call starts with a notice that it may be recorded.

Does it replace my receptionist?

No. If a human can answer, they should. It takes the calls at lunch, after six, on Saturday, and when the trucks are all out.

What if it gets something wrong?

It only quotes what you've given it: hours, services, price ranges, policies. Anything outside that becomes a call back or a transfer, never a guess. You get the transcript of every call, and we tune it from them.

Does it work with my calendar and software?

Google Calendar and Google Workspace out of the box. Other calendars and CRMs on the Pro plan. Your existing phone number stays; we add forwarding on no-answer.

What does it cost per minute?

You never see a per-minute bill inside a level. The month's total minutes put you in Starter, Growth or Pro, and that is the fee. Only beyond 4,500 minutes in a month, roughly fifty calls a day, is there a per-minute rate of 25 cents. Every invoice shows the minutes and calls it is based on.

What is the sign-up fee for?

The one-time $425 covers building the receptionist around your business: your hours, services, prices and the questions callers ask, then testing it with you and setting up call forwarding. Nothing goes live until you have called it yourself and approved it.

How long does set-up take?

About a week: a 30-minute call to collect your hours, services and the questions callers ask, then testing with you before it goes live.
